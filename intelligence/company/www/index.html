Why Meridian

Meridian is not a generic personal assistant runtime. It is a governance-first research platform for local AI agents where authority, budget, and sanctions are explicit and inspectable.

Governed by design

Institution policy, Authority warrants, Treasury rules, and Court actions are modeled as first-class runtime constraints.

Locally sovereign

Execution and state stay local by default, with explicit boundaries for hosted surfaces and external adapters.

Research-ready surface

PoGE receipts, runtime proof routes, and transparent constraints are exposed for reproducible governance experiments.

Governance Model

Meridian runs five governance primitives and a three-ledger economy in one coherent runtime contract.

Institution
Charter

Defines policy defaults, lifecycle, and owner accountability.

Authority
Warrants

Routes high-risk actions through explicit approval paths.

Treasury
Discipline

Enforces reserve-floor controls, spend checks, and payout boundaries.

Court
Sanctions

Records violations, appeals, and remediation with auditable history.
