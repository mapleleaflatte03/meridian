## 2024-05-24 - [List Comprehension vs Generator Expression in CPython]
**Learning:** An optimization that surprisingly DIDN'T work: replacing `len([x for x in list if cond])` with `sum(1 for x in list if cond)`. While the generator expression avoids creating an intermediate list in memory, it is measurably slower (1.4x pessimization) in CPython because the list comprehension utilizes the highly optimized C-level `LIST_APPEND` opcode, whereas `sum()` incurs per-iteration Python-level generator overhead.
**Action:** Do not "optimize" list comprehensions to generator expressions simply to avoid intermediate allocations unless memory is a proven, critical bottleneck for that specific path. The intermediate list overhead is generally cheaper than the generator overhead in CPython.
